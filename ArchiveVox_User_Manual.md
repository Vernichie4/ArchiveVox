## Section 1: Getting Started

### 1.1 System Requirements

To use ArchiveVox, you need:

**For Teachers, Principals, and Admins:**
- A computer with internet access
- A modern web browser (Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge, or Safari)
- A valid username and password provided by your school administrator

**For Students:**
- A computer, tablet, or smartphone with internet access
- A modern web browser
- A working microphone for recording reading assessments
- A valid username and password provided by your teacher

**Technical Requirements:**
- The application is web-based and runs in the browser
- No software installation is required on your device
- The application works on Windows, Mac, and mobile devices

---

### 1.2 Accessing the Application

**Step 1:** Open your web browser

**Step 2:** Enter the ArchiveVox URL provided by your school administrator

**Step 3:** The login screen will appear

**Note:** If you do not know the URL, contact your school's IT administrator or teacher.

---

### 1.3 Login and Logout

#### Logging In

**Step 1:** On the login screen, enter your username in the "Username" field

**Step 2:** Enter your password in the "Password" field

**Step 3:** Click the "Sign In" button

### 4.4 Understanding the 3-Attempt System

Each reading assignment allows a maximum of 3 attempts.

#### 5.4.1 Maximum 3 Attempts

- **Attempt 1:** First time you read the material
- **Attempt 2:** Second time you read the material
- **Attempt 3:** Third and final time you read the material
- **Attempt 4+:** Not allowed - assignment locks

The assignment card shows how many attempts you have used (e.g., "1/3 attempts", "2/3 attempts").

#### 5.4.2 Practice vs. Final Score Attempts

**Practice Attempts (1 and 2):**
- These are for practice
- Your score is not sent for final grading
- Labeled as "Practice Attempt"
- Helps you prepare without pressure

**Final Score Attempt (3):**
- This is your official attempt
- Your score is sent to your teacher for grading
- Labeled as "Final Assessment Recorded"
- Counts toward your final grade

#### 5.4.3 Using "Try Again" for Re-recording

After completing a practice attempt (1 or 2), you can re-record without using another attempt:

**Step 1:** After submitting your reading, view your results

**Step 2:** If it was a practice attempt, you will see a "Try Again" button
- Button shows: "🔄 Try Again (X attempts left)"

**Step 3:** Click "Try Again"

**Step 4:** You return to the recording screen
- The attempt number does NOT increase
- You can re-record your reading
- This does not consume an additional attempt

**Step 5:** Record again and submit

**Step 6:** Your new recording replaces the previous one
- Still counts as the same attempt number
- Still a practice attempt (if attempt 1 or 2)

**Figure 5.3: Student Reading Results with Try Again Button**
*[SCREENSHOT: 19_student_try_again.png]*

**Important:** The "Try Again" feature is only available for practice attempts (1 and 2). After your final attempt (3), you cannot re-record.

#### 5.4.4 When Assignments Lock

When you have used all 3 attempts:
- The assignment locks
- The Start button is disabled
- Shows "🔒 Locked (3 attempts reached)"
- You cannot record or take the quiz again

**If you need more attempts:**
- Contact your teacher
- Your teacher may reset your attempts in special cases

## Section 6: Reading Assessment Metrics Explained

### 6.1 WCPM (Words Correct Per Minute)

**What it measures:**
- How many words you read correctly in one minute
- Your reading speed
- Fluency

**How it's calculated:**
- Total number of words read correctly ÷ time in minutes

**What it means:**
- Higher WCPM = faster, more fluent reading
- Lower WCPM = slower, less fluent reading
- Target WCPM varies by grade level

**Example:**
- If you read 120 words correctly in 1 minute, your WCPM is 120

---

### 6.2 Accuracy Percentage

**What it measures:**
- How many words you read correctly
- Overall reading accuracy
- Precision

**How it's calculated:**
- (Words read correctly ÷ Total words) × 100

**What it means:**
- 90-100% = Excellent accuracy
- 80-89% = Good accuracy
- 70-79% = Fair accuracy
- Below 70% = Needs improvement

**Example:**
- If a passage has 100 words and you read 85 correctly, your accuracy is 85%

---

### 6.3 Miscue Types

A miscue is a deviation from the exact text. Not all miscues are errors - some are natural parts of reading.

#### 6.3.1 Substitutions

**Definition:** You say a different word than what is written

**Example:**
- Text: "The cat sat on the mat"
- You say: "The dog sat on the mat"
- "Dog" is a substitution for "cat"

**Impact:** Can change meaning if the substituted word has a different meaning

#### 6.3.2 Omissions

**Definition:** You skip or don't read a word

**Example:**
- Text: "The cat sat on the mat"
- You say: "The cat sat on mat"
- "The" before "mat" is an omission

**Impact:** Can affect comprehension and fluency

#### 6.3.3 Insertions

**Definition:** You add a word that is not in the text

**Example:**
- Text: "The cat sat on the mat"
- You say: "The big cat sat on the mat"
- "Big" is an insertion

**Impact:** Can affect fluency and rhythm

#### 6.3.4 Repetitions

**Definition:** You repeat a word or phrase

**Example:**
- Text: "The cat sat on the mat"
- You say: "The cat cat sat on the mat"
- "Cat" is repeated

**Impact:** Can affect fluency and speed

---

### 6.4 Reading Levels (CRLA Profile)

The CRLA (Critical Reading Level Assessment) profile categorizes readers into three levels:

**Independent Level:**
- You can read and understand without help
- Accuracy: 95-100%
- Can read independently

**Instructional Level:**
- You can read with some guidance
- Accuracy: 90-94%
- Benefits from teacher support

**Frustration Level:**
- Reading is difficult and frustrating
- Accuracy: Below 90%
- Needs significant support and easier materials

**Observation Level:**
- More detailed categorization within levels
- Helps teachers provide targeted instruction

---

### 6.5 Fluency Scores

Fluency combines:
- **Speed (WCPM):** How fast you read
- **Accuracy:** How correctly you read
- **Expression:** How well you read with appropriate tone and emotion

**Good Fluency:**
- Reads at an appropriate pace
- Reads accurately
- Reads with expression
- Comprehends what is read

**Needs Improvement:**
- Reads too slowly or too quickly
- Makes many errors
- Reads in a monotone voice
- Struggles with comprehension

**Problem:** Cannot log in

**Solutions:**
- Check that your username and password are correct
- Make sure Caps Lock is not on
- Contact your teacher or administrator if you forgot your password
- Ensure you have internet connection

**Problem:** Account locked

**Solutions:**
- Wait a few minutes and try again
- Contact your administrator to unlock your account

---

### 7.2 Audio Recording Problems

**Problem:** Microphone not working

**Solutions:**
- Check that your microphone is plugged in
- Allow microphone access in your browser
- Check browser settings for microphone permissions
- Try a different browser
- Test your microphone in other applications

**Problem:** Recording quality is poor

**Solutions:**
- Move closer to the microphone
- Reduce background noise
- Speak clearly and at a normal volume
- Check your microphone for damage

---

### 7.3 OCR Extraction Errors

**Problem:** OCR does not extract text correctly

**Solutions:**
- Ensure the image is clear and high-quality
- Make sure the text is readable in the image
- Use the editing feature to correct the text manually
- Try uploading a different image

**Problem:** OCR is taking too long

**Solutions:**
- Wait a few more seconds - OCR can take time
- Check your internet connection
- Try uploading a smaller image file

---

### 7.4 Upload Failures

**Problem:** Cannot upload material image

**Solutions:**
- Check that the file format is supported (JPG, PNG, PDF)
- Ensure the file size is not too large
- Check your internet connection
- Try a different file

**Problem:** Upload error message

**Solutions:**
- Read the error message carefully
- Check that all required fields are filled
- Try refreshing the page and uploading again
- Contact technical support if the problem persists

---

### 7.5 Quiz Submission Issues

**Problem:** Cannot submit quiz

**Solutions:**
- Ensure all questions are answered
- Check your internet connection
- Try refreshing the page
- Contact your teacher if the problem continues

**Problem:** Quiz score not showing

**Solutions:**
- Wait a few seconds for the score to calculate
- Refresh the page
- Check your Progress view for the score

### 7.6 Contacting Support

If you cannot resolve an issue:

**For Students:**
- Contact your teacher first
- Your teacher can escalate to technical support if needed

**For Teachers:**
- Contact your school's IT administrator
- Check with other teachers if they have the same issue

**For Principals/Admins:**
- Contact your school's IT department

## Glossary

**Accuracy Percentage:** The percentage of words read correctly out of the total words in a passage.

**Admin:** A school administrator with full access to all features, including school-wide student and material management.

**Assessment:** A reading evaluation that measures a student's reading fluency, accuracy, and comprehension.

**Assignment:** A reading task assigned by a teacher to students, which may include one or more reading materials.

**Attempt:** One complete reading of a material, including recording and possibly a quiz. Students have a maximum of 3 attempts per assignment.

**CRLA Profile:** Critical Reading Level Assessment - a system for categorizing readers into Independent, Instructional, or Frustration levels.

**Final Score Attempt:** The third attempt in the 3-attempt system, which is officially graded and sent to the teacher.

**Fluency:** The ability to read text accurately, quickly, and with appropriate expression.

**Insertion:** A miscue where the reader adds a word that is not in the text.

**LRN:** Learner Reference Number - a unique identifier for each student.

**Miscue:** A deviation from the exact text while reading. Miscues include substitutions, omissions, insertions, and repetitions.

**OCR:** Optical Character Recognition - technology that converts images of text into machine-readable text.

**Omission:** A miscue where the reader skips or does not read a word.

**Practice Attempt:** The first or second attempt in the 3-attempt system, used for practice and not officially graded.

**Principal:** A school administrator who can view school-wide analytics and manage teachers, but has limited access to student and material management.

**Reading Level:** A categorization of a reader's ability (Independent, Instructional, or Frustration) based on accuracy and comprehension.

**Repetition:** A miscue where the reader repeats a word or phrase.

**Substitution:** A miscue where the reader says a different word than what is written.

**Teacher:** A classroom instructor who manages students, materials, assignments, and conducts assessments.

**Try Again:** A feature that allows students to re-record their reading without consuming an additional attempt (available only for practice attempts).

**WCPM:** Words Correct Per Minute - a measure of reading speed calculated by dividing the number of words read correctly by the time in minutes.
